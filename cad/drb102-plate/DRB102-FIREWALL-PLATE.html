1. How it goes together

Plate sits on the engine side of the firewall. 10× M5 screws come from the cabin side through the firewall into blind tapped holes in the plate. The O-ring groove is on the plate's firewall face. The DRBF-1A flange + DRB12 receptacle hang on the cabin side of the plate, inside the firewall hole (TE: receptacle snaps into the flange from its side; flange screws go in from the plug side). The DRB16 plug mates from the engine side and is driven home with an M8 socket on its center jackscrew.

5. O-ring

SizeAS568-261 (ID 6.734" / 171.04 mm, W 0.139" / 3.53 mm). Groove inner perimeter is 1% over the ring ID (21.37" / 542.7 mm), so the ring stretches ~1% onto the inner wall. Molded ring, no splice.
Why inner wallWater/oil come from the engine side, outside the ring (pressure inward). Parker: size the groove on its ID for inward pressure, +1% max.
GlandParker Design Chart 4-3 face seal, 1/8" cord: depth .101–.107" (used 2.64), width liquids .177–.187" (used 4.70). Squeeze 21–29%, fill ≈79%.
MaterialFKM (Viton) 75A — handles oil, fuel mist and firewall heat. EPDM 70A is fine if no oil exposure. Lightly grease to hold it in the groove during install.
Sources: Parker Design Chart 4-3 face seal glands · Parker Design Table 4-2 (AS568 sizes)

8. Design choices

Plate on engine side, screws from cabinMatches “screw from cabin side” with tapped plate holes; plug side is wide open for the socket.
12.7 plate + 6.0 web pocketTE max panel is 6.35; the thick rim gives M5 thread depth with a solid floor.
Pocket on engine face, not firewall faceKeeps the firewall face flat for the O-ring and the TE flange seal.
M5, 10 screws~62–67 mm spacing keeps even O-ring squeeze; M6 would need a thicker plate for the same floor.
Blind holesNo leak path through the plate.
AS568-261 molded ringStandard size fits the path; no splice joint to leak.
R10/R12 cornersCut with a Ø12 end mill in one pass; groove corners ≥3× cord for the O-ring.
Firewall hole 155×105Clears the flange body with ~3 mm, still leaves 5+ mm to the O-ring.

9. Gaps and things to check

Not on TE drawingsPlug M8 hex protrusion length, mated stack height vs panel, flange boss depth for screw length. The envelopes in the assembly start at the panel faces (conservative). Check against a real part or TE's 3D models (linked on the product pages).
Key notch positionDrawn as R4.32 centered on the cutout's bottom edge, read from the drawing geometry (only the radius is dimensioned). Confirm against the DRBF-1A key before cutting.
Fit on the carPlate is 201 × 150. Measure the ST185 firewall area and the engine-side room: plug 45 mm + socket and harness bend.
Firewall thicknessModeled as 1.0 mm reference only. Measure it to set M5 screw length.
