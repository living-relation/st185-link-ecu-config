How it goes together

POWER box (3 printed pieces)

  1. Press an M6 limiter into each of the 4 feet.
  2. Base right way up: lay the bar and the 14 pads in their pockets. From below, push the M5 x 16 bolts (and the M8 x 25 for the 1/0) up through the hex head pockets and the bar holes. Slide a 40 mm standoff up each hex hole (both rows) and screw a set screw down through each pad into it.
  3. Turn the base over. Crimp the relay terminals, push each one up into its cavity until it clicks, and tie the wires into their bundles (they leave the inboard end). Bolt the AMI output lugs under their standoffs (M5 x 10, both rows); each relay feed goes lug -> straight -> round its saddle -> terminal 30; the OUT cables run straight out under the skirt.
  4. Right way up: AMI fuses on the studs, Keps nuts 4 Nm max; 1/0 lug on the M8.
  5. Mount the base by its 4 feet, snap the belly pan up into the skirt, plug the relays and the ACPS fuse in, snap the cover on.

LOGIC box (3 printed pieces)

  1. Limiters in the feet. Make the 19 jumpers: 14 AWG, about 60 mm, a 880398-2 receptacle on one end, a PIDG 53941-1 ring lug on the other.
  2. Wire side up: push each jumper receptacle up into its fuse input cavity, and the output / micro-relay terminals into theirs; tie each column's wires into its bundle (front edge).
  3. Slide the two strips (on edge, lug face toward their jumpers) in from the rear along the spine lips. Bolt each jumper lug to its stud (M4 x 8 + spring washer) and each feed lug to its strip (M5 x 10).
  4. Mount by the 4 feet (long sides), snap the pan on, fuses and micro relays in, snap the cover on.

Battery block (2 printed pieces)

  1. From below, an M6 nut into each of the 2 hex pockets under the relay platform; M6 limiters in the end tabs (L 6) and the 2 flange bosses (L 4).
  2. Drop the 3 bolt heads into the fuse pedestal pockets (top), lay the bar and the pad on.
  3. HCR150 on its platform, 2 M6 bolts down through the flange into the captured nuts.
  4. AMI 40 on, jumper (two lugs, one straight 18 mm cable) from the AMI out to terminal 3, pump cable on terminal 5, coil plug on.
  5. Mount by the 2 end tabs (passenger side of the trunk, between the rear wall and the battery - Z4), snap the cover on.

To service a terminal later: unsnap the pan - every cavity is right there.

Bend radius check (every cable at every lug, stud and terminal)

Rule: FAA AC 43.13-1B CHG 1, par. 11-96 aa (p. 11-44/45) - a bundle no tighter than 10 x the OD of its largest wire; where a wire breaks out at a terminal and is suitably supported, 3 x OD. No cable maker publishes a bend radius for M22759/16 Tefzel or for SGX battery cable (checked: Thermax / ProWire M22759/16 sheet, Southwire SPEC 50050, AWC, Prestolite), so this installation rule is used. OD = maximum from the Thermax M22759/16 sheet; 1/0 SGX 13.6 mm.
