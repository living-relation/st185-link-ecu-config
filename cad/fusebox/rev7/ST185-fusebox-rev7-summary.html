What changed from rev 6

  1. No copper bar of its own in the POWER box. The bus bar's + bar (POS) runs the whole length: 1" x 1/4" tinned copper, 226 mm long (rev 6 POWER bar: 148). The 14 AMI positions bolt onto it exactly as in rev 6 (same pads, standoffs, relays, saddles, harness bundles).
  2. A bus zone at the back of the box (+62 mm deep). On the + bar: BAT+ IN, RADLOK+ and a spare M8 - those cables leave straight out of the outboard end. Beside it a NEG bar (same stock, 105 mm): BAT-, RADLOK-, GND ID, GND IG and one M8 for the small relay grounds - those leave straight out the back.
  3. A printed wall between NEG and POS (2.5 mm, 20 mm above the deck). Closest NEG part to a POS part: 5.8 mm, with the wall in between.
  4. B rev3 adapted to R76 (cut + drill only): its machined aluminium bars and 1/0 set-screw port blocks become plain tinned copper bars with M8 bolts (head captured in the deck, like every other bolt) and Klauke 6R8 lugs. Its universal bar U and the link strap / AMG fuse option are dropped (question U1 below).
  5. Feet: still four with M6 limiters; the rear-inboard foot moved round to the inboard end so no cable runs over a mounting bolt.
  6. Unchanged: LOGIC box and battery relay block (their feeds do not move); bend radius rule, belly pan, PA12 print rules, M6 limiters, 40 mm standoffs.
