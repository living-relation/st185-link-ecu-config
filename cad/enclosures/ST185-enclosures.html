ST185 custom enclosures

2 x VRC box (front + rear, same design) and 1 x CSB3 box. Machined 6061-T6, O-ring lid seal, countersunk M3 lid screws, slotted tabs. Draft for Dan, 2026-10-13.

Dan's answers (DECISIONS S26) and what is still open
  • E1 Answered: a 3D-printed holder grabs the VR board on the box floor. No carrier board, no standoffs. Simplified 2026-10-16 (Dan M9, DECISIONS R126, replaces the v1 holder): one extruded PA12 block, board flush in a pocket with 4 snap tabs, wires soldered on the board top, 2 x M6 countersunk screws into 2 floor bosses - see "VRC board holder" below.
  • E2 Answered: the board is the OpenLogicEFI Dual VR Conditioner, DetonationEMS version. The holder is sized from the maker's KiCad file (RevB3, 24.13 x 24.10 mm); check your board against it when it arrives.
  • E4 Answered: NBR (Parker N0674-70 2-039).
  • E5 Answered: M6 nylon tab screws; the slots are 6.6 wide.
  • Check on a real LP-16 socket: rear nut size (not on the drawing). The inside D30 counterbore is a safe allowance.

VRC board holder (one per box)

Holder v2 with the board in it (board from the OpenLogicEFI KiCad file, no header: wires solder on its top).

Whatv2 (Dan M9, 2026-10-16, DECISIONS R126 - replaces the v1 holder): ONE extruded PA12 block, powder bed like the fuse boxes (R73: walls 2.0 except the 4 snap tabs). 57.7 x 35.2 x 13.4 mm over the tab lips, 21.2 cm3 (R131, 2026-10-16: 2.5 higher, board top z 17.5, lead reliefs at both ends under the lowest LP-16 cups; 0.47 to the LP-16 rears, 21.5 board top to lid). Dan: "Redesign to be simpler. wires soldered from top of pcb. extrude solid block. cut square recess for the pcb with little tabs to hold it down."
BoardOpenLogicEFI Dual VR Conditioner NCV1124 RevB3 (Duel VR.kicad_pcb): 24.13 x 24.10 x 1.6, corner R0.71, no holes, SMD on top. No header: the leads solder into its pin holes from the top. Pocket = board + 0.4 per side, 1.6 deep, so the board top is flush with the block top (z 15); two 1.5 mm reliefs under the pin rows take the solder tips.
Board retention4 snap tabs on the long pocket sides (1.0 arm, 7 mm free, freed by a slot and two slits; 0.6 lip with a lead-in, 0.1 over the board), clear of the NCV1124. Press the board in; lift a tab to take it out.
Fixing2 x M6 x 14 ISO 10642 A2 TX30 countersunk screws (Lemu 2990806014; R131, was M6 x 12 RS PRO 266-8419) in opposite corners, flush in 90 deg D12.2 countersinks (D6.6 clearance), into 2 bosses on the box floor: D11 x 4.5 high, drill D5.0 x 8.5 deep, tap M6x1.0 later (7.5 full thread, 1.0 floor left, sealed); 6.5 mm of thread engaged, 2.5 mm of PA12 clamped over the boss. The holder sits on the floor with the bosses in recesses in its underside. The 4 M3 floor bosses of the v1 holder are removed (suppressed in Fusion).
LeadsPins 1-4 (VR inputs) to the IN socket, 5-8 (VDD, GND, outputs) to the OUT socket. Leads M22759/16-20 (OD 1.57), bends 3 x OD (FAA AC 43.13-1B 11-96): out of the cup, over the block and straight down onto the board-top pad; none touches the box, holder, board or sockets; closest lead pair 0.94 mm (the 2.54 pad pitch allows 0.97); OUT 1 / OUT 3 pass over the other two because the OUT map crosses over. The drain jumper (IN 3 to OUT 5) runs over the block.

O-ring groove

  • Face groove in the body rim: 2.65 wide (2.57-2.72) x 1.32 deep (1.27-1.37), bottom radius 0.13-0.38. Parker ORD 5700 Design Chart 4-3, 1.78 mm ring, liquids.
  • Corner radius on the groove centreline is R10.7 = 6 x ring thickness (Parker 4.3: at least 3x, ideally 6x).
  • Both boxes use the same groove length (229.6 mm), so one O-ring size covers all three boxes: AS568-039, about 2.4 % stretch, 26 % squeeze, 71 % groove fill.
  • Lid closes metal-to-metal on the rim. The 4 screws sit in the corners, outside the O-ring.

Machining notes

  • 3-axis mill. Body setup 1: outside, tabs and slots from the bottom. Setup 2: cavity (R7.7 corners, D12 cutter or smaller), groove (D2 cutter, two passes), tapped holes. Setup 3 (VRC): both end walls, D-holes.
  • VRC inside D30 counterbores sit on the inside end walls and stay as modelled (Dan, 2026-10-14: the boxes may be 3D printed or machined, either can do it). 3D printed: as modelled, no extra step. Machined: a back-counterbore tool through the D-hole, or a second setup on that end wall (right-angle head).
  • Ra 0.8 on the groove, the lid underside and the CSB3 flange seat. Lid underside flat to 0.05.
  • Threads M3x0.5-6H, 8 deep in the rim (drill 2.5 x 9.5 + 118 deg). Holder bosses (R126, F02 + N4 R131 2026-10-16): D11, top z 12.5, drill D5.0 x 11.5 deep, tap M6x1.0 (10.0 full thread, 9.0 engaged = 1.5 x D) at fit - 1.0 mm of floor left, no through holes. CSB3 standoff holes: drill 2.5 x 6.0 + point, M3 x 4.5, with an M3 x 8 set screw as the stud (F03).
  • Clear anodize is fine: nothing bonds to the case (DECISIONS R31/R33). Keep the groove and seal faces uncoated or anodize-only.
  • BREAK SHARP EDGES 0.2-0.5 EXCEPT O-RING / SEAL FACES. Floor-to-wall inside corners: cutter corner OK (square end mill). Tab-to-body corners R3; CSB3 tab corners at x ±30 R1.9 - cutter r ≤ 1.5 there. Tab slots 6.6 x 10. Engraved IN / OUT on the VRC ends: Arial Black 4.0, 0.3 deep, upright and readable from outside (strokes about 1.2, a normal engraving cutter).
