Charge lamp LED test

Toyota Celica ST185 (3S-GTE, 4WD) - OEM IC-regulator alternator, 12 V plug-in LED in place of the charge warning bulb. Question: can the LED alone do the bulb's job? The 56 Ω resistor r_chg_excite was taken off ST185-ClusterLED on 2026-09-28 without waiting for this test, so run it at first start to confirm.

Expected answer: yes, the LED alone should work. Both the 1990 ST185 EWD (Charging, p.52) and the 1993 Celica wiring diagrams (Fig. 1 and Fig. 15) show the IC regulator powered from its own ignition lead (IG, BLK-RED). The charge lamp sits between the IGN fuse (cluster C12 pin 9) and the alternator L terminal (C12 pin 8, YEL) with a diode in series inside the cluster. The regulator only pulls L to ground to light the lamp, so the lamp current is not what excites the field. This test confirms that on the car.

Before you start

  • Tools: digital multimeter (DC volts), back-probe pins, a helper to hold 2000 rpm, a thermometer or a warm engine.
  • Battery: charged, resting voltage 12.4 V or more. Record it in step 0.
  • No resistor: the harness no longer has r_chg_excite. Only the LED sits in the lamp position.
  • Measure to a clean engine or battery ground. Back-probe connectors. Don't pierce insulation.

Procedure

0

Battery resting voltage

Engine off, key off, lights off for a few minutes. Measure across the battery posts.

Record it. 12.4 V or more to continue.

1

LED polarity

LED anode (+) on the C12 pin 9 side (BLK-ORG, IGN feed). Cathode (−) towards C12 pin 8 (YEL, alternator L). The cluster's own series diode stays in place.

2

IG feed - ignition ON, engine off

BLK-RED at the alternator plug to ground.

Spec: about battery voltage (within 0.5 V of the step 0 reading).

3

L terminal - ignition ON, engine off

YEL at the alternator plug to ground, and look at the LED.

Spec: 0-4 V and the LED is lit (1990 ST185 EWD service hint).

If L is near battery voltage and the LED is dark, the regulator is not pulling L low, or the LED is reversed or open. Stop and refit the resistor.

4

Start and idle - no revving

Start the engine and leave it at idle. Watch the LED, then measure the battery after about 30 s.

Spec: LED goes out, and the battery reads higher than the step 0 resting voltage (charging at idle, without a rev to self-excite).

5

Regulated voltage - 2000 rpm, warm

Hold 2000 rpm, electrical loads off. WHT (S) at the alternator plug to ground.

Spec: 13.9-15.1 V at 25 °C (13.5-14.3 V at 115 °C). LED stays off.

6

Key off

Switch off. The LED must go dark (no faint glow).

Result

Fill in the readings above.
  • All pass: the LED alone is enough. Nothing to change.
  • Any fail: fit a 56 Ω 5 W resistor across the LED (C12 pin 9 to pin 8) as a temporary fix, keep the readings, and send them over. Note which step failed.
