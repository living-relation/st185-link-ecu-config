  DOES THE SHADOWING PENALTY APPLY WHEN THE RADIATOR ACTUALLY MATTERS?
    sustained WOT climb, 100 km/h      intercooler rejects   55.4 kW | radiator sees  70.0 C | capacity  12.6 of  28.5 kW (55.8% lost)
    part throttle recovery, 100 km/h   intercooler rejects    4.2 kW | radiator sees  38.3 C | capacity  25.9 of  28.5 kW ( 9.2% lost)

    -> the penalty is large only while on boost, and while on boost the
       coolant load is an order of magnitude above what any ST185 radiator
       can shed anyway. On the recovery lap, where the radiator decides
       whether the session continues, the intercooler is rejecting almost
       nothing and the shadowing penalty nearly vanishes.
