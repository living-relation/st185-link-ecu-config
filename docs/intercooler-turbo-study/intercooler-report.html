03When to spray

Spray only where T2/IAT and cylinder pressure need it. 93 gates earliest (knock). E42 in the middle. E85 last (compressor-outlet heat at high PR × high flow). Cold IAT and part-throttle stay dry so the tank lasts and the charge is not over-wet at idle. Green cells are spray-on at WOT / hot IAT. Do not spray in vacuum, cruise, or cold charge.

ECU control — digital output, no pin assigned

GP Output → relay coil (low-side Aux or switchboard output when one is free). Aux 1–10 are all taken in XTREMEX-IO-TABLE.html. Do not steal a Digital Input for the pump (use a free DI later for tank empty — do not take a live DI).

  1. GP Output → relay coil for an on/off or PWM pump. Prefer a switchboard digital output when one exists so the XtremeX Aux map stays untouched. If a low-side Aux later frees, that works too.
  2. Conditions (three GP Output tables, or one table with ethanol% as a 4D axis once flex is trusted): MAP ≥ gate, RPM ≥ gate, TPS ≥ 80%, IAT ≥ gate, AND a user-enable (dash or virtual Aux).
  3. Hardware: 50/50 water / methanol, post-IC, pre-throttle, check valve, arming switch. Fail-safe = off if the output is faulted.
  4. A free DI later can be tank-empty / flow-switch AND-inhibit only. Do not assign that DI now — DI 8–10 stay uncommitted.

05Bar-plate vs tube-and-fin

Same 27×12 in face, same 55 lb/min charge, same 60 mph stack airflow. The difference is the core, not the size. Effectiveness is calibrated into MatchBot's official "typical IC" high-speed band (85–90%). Thermal mass and recovery come from aluminum volume × Cp / UA.

Road car: most time is part-throttle. Tube-and-fin has ~half the metal mass of bar-and-plate at the same 4.5 in / 27x12 in face, so recovery at speed is faster and heat-soak after a pull-then-traffic is shorter. Steady-state ε is a few points lower; we buy that back with 4.5 in thickness (allowed). Bar-plate wins a 95% WOT duty cycle (time-attack, drag) where the extra UA stays useful and soak never has time to matter. Charge dP is also lower on tube-fin, which is compressor efficiency the turbo does not have to pay.

Idle / traffic soak (4.5 in)

Bar-plate 8.81 kg vs tube-fin 4.72 kg. Recovery τ at 60 mph is 4.6 s tube-fin vs 5.4 s bar-plate — the road-car number that matters.

What 4–5 in buys

3 in tube-fin drops out of the 85% band on this face at 55 lb/min. 4.0–4.5 in is where tube-fin enters it. 5 in still helps bar-plate more than tube-fin (diminishing NTU). 4.5 in is the pick: fits the crash-bar depth.

06Fins per inch

Pro Alloy: 12–14 FPI for a typical IC so the radiator still sees air; denser pitches choke the stack. Tube-fin at 12 FPI is the street/debris pick (16+ is race, clogs). Bar-plate already runs a denser internal pack — 10 FPI air-side keeps the 4.5 in core from starving the Mishimoto rad. PRL: higher FPI is more UA and more dP; we are UA-rich at 4.5 in.

Tube-fin (this pick) — 12 FPI

Street/track default for extruded-tube cores. 14 FPI is a drag-only step when the water radiator is dedicated and you do not care about the OEM fan. Below 10 FPI you give away charge cooling the 7163 still needs at 55 lb/min.

Bar-plate — 10 FPI

Bar-plate already has more internal surface per inch of thickness. Packing 14–16 FPI in front of this radiator is how the 4.5 in bricks overheat the water. 12 FPI bar-plate is acceptable only if the core is thinner (2.0–2.5 in) and offset from the rad.

Sources: Pro Alloy, Intercooler Cores Explained — 12–14 FPI typical; PRL Motorsports, Intercooler Fin Design — FPI vs cooling/dP.

07Front stack

Order, front to back: bumper / crash bar, FMIC (4.5 in tube-fin, 27 x 12 in face), oil cooler (low, off the rad face if possible), trans cooler (same), half-width AC condenser, Mishimoto racing radiator + slim fans.

Half-width condenser

60 mph radiator air: half-width 2.893 kg/s (1.11× need) vs full-width 2.696 kg/s (1.04×). The half-width unit sits passenger-side so the driver-side rad face sees ram air.

Radiator + fans

Mishimoto 2-row racing (Celica / 5S-class), slim PWM pair behind. Two-row aluminum, Celica / 5S-class. Slim PWM pair behind the core. Oil and trans coolers live off the rad face.

08Pipes — 2.50 in both sides

At 55.13 lb/min the 2.50 in hot side is 228 ft/s — inside Garrett's 200–300 ft/s band. The 2.50 in cold side is 174 ft/s, under the band because the charge is denser after the IC, but dP is 0.65 psi and the pipe is not a restriction versus the 7163's 60 lb/min wall or the engine's 55 lb/min demand. 3.00 in cold saves ~0.36 psi and adds 2.0 L plus ~14 ms of fill delay. That volume is why 3 in felt slower. Both sides stay 2.50 in × 0.065 wall.
