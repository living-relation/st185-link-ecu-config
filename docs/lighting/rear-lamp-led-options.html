Rear Lamp LED Options

Reference page, written 2026-10-18 (Claude Code, spare-4). Settled by DECISIONS R117 (lamp ends soldered to the factory pigtails), R177 (heat shrink sized per end) and R178 (LED flasher). Scope: the lamps on ST185-RearLights: rear combination lamps R11 / R12 (tail, stop, turn, back-up, rear side marker), licence light L1, high-mount stop light H6.

Bottom line

  • No validated no-solder path exists. The factory socket-and-wire assemblies are discontinued, I could not name the factory plug that mates with them, and no aftermarket twist-lock socket is shown to fit the ST185 housings. A sealed crimp splice (TE D-406) takes one gauge band on each end, and nothing validated bridges the 16 AWG project wire to a thinner pigtail. The lamps stay soldered (R117, sleeve sizing per R177) unless LEDs are chosen; LED bulbs plug into the existing sockets and change nothing in the wiring.
  • LED bulbs are easy. Every lamp uses a common base (1157, 1156, T10 wedge), so plug-in LED bulbs from Sylvania ZEVO or Diode Dynamics fit the existing sockets. No socket-replacement board or LED lamp exists for the ST185 (every LED lamp I found is for the 2000-2005 Celica).
  • Hyper-flash is settled (R178): the stock flasher T3 in J/B No.1 is replaced by the load-independent electronic flasher CF13JL-02, so LED turn bulbs need no load resistors (section 5).
  • Not stated by any maker: DOT / SAE marking. None of the Sylvania, Philips or Diode Dynamics pages I read claims it.

  • Polarity: Sylvania ZEVO, DD HP11 and DD HP3 pages all say non-polar, so polarity is not a fit issue on these.
  • Heat: LEDs cut lamp-socket heat (27 W to a few watts), which helps the old brittle sockets. Fit risk is the bulb length in the housing, which no maker page states.
  • Colour: a white LED behind a red lens looks pink (Sylvania FAQ), so use red LEDs behind red lens sections (stop, tail, HMSL, rear marker) and white only behind clear back-up lenses.
  • DOT / SAE: not stated by any page read. A bulb swap is not a DOT-listed lamp, so treat road legality as unconfirmed.
  • Hyper-flash (turn signals only): settled, see section 5 (R178). Back-up, stop, tail, marker and licence lamps are not on the flasher.

5. Hyper-flash fix (settled): flasher CF13JL-02 in T3, J/B No.1 (DECISIONS R178)

Decided 2026-10-18 (R178): an LED-compatible electronic flasher goes in the stock flasher socket instead of load resistors. Only the turn signals need it; tail, stop, back-up, marker, licence and high-mount stop are not on the flasher.

  • Stock flasher T3: Toyota 81980-12070 (now 81980-32010, Denso), the top relay in J/B No.1 behind the LH kick panel. 1993 EWD pp. 130 - 131: pin 2 = +12 V (B), pin 1 = output (L), pin 3 = ground (E). The hazard runs through it, so one flasher serves turn and hazard.
  • Replacement: Super Bright LEDs CF13JL-02 (3-pin Japanese type, $9.99): 11 - 15 V, 0.02 - 20 A, 150 W max, 60 - 120 per minute, -40 to +80 C, 2-year warranty, load independent. Its maker wiring diagram shows B+ / L / E. No click sound (the maker suggests an output relay). Buy line: docs/sourcing/README.md.
  • Source status: maker page plus the EWD; the unit is a generic product sold under many names, so there is no independent datasheet for the pin geometry.

Availability: the exact 1157R / 1156R packs are on Sylvania's site and at retailers; I found no Amazon or AliExpress listing for any part number above with usable search results (searches returned none), and no eBay listing for an ST185 socket. Search links: eBay 81555-2B090, Amazon ZEVO 1157R, AliExpress 1157 LED (search pages, not listings; listing specs are not used). OEM bulb prices at toyotapartsdeal.com: 99132-21270 about $0.82, 99132-11270 about $1.30, 99132-12050 about $3.56 per pack of 10.
