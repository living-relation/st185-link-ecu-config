Firewall bulkhead: smarter options

Proposals only. Nothing in the repo or on harness.design was changed. I read the repo from origin/main (commit 355ac7a) through a temporary copy. I skipped git pull so I wouldn't touch the working tree while another task is editing. Local main is 1 commit behind, and that commit only changes docs/research-hub.html.

My recommendation: one Deutsch DRB 102-way rectangular bulkhead (DRB12-102PAE-L018 + DRB16-102SAE-L018 + DRBF-1A flange). It replaces both HDP circulars and the DTP 4-way.
  • One firewall hole instead of three. The receptacle is 125.8 × 75.3 mm.
  • Everything fits, with room to spare: 80 size-16, 16 size-12, 4 size-8 and 2 size-4 positions. You need 54 + 4, which leaves 26 size-16 (33%) and 12 size-12 spare, plus 6 heavy-power positions.
  • Reuses contacts you already own: gold size-16 (0460-202-1631 / 0462-201-1631) for every signal, gold size-12 (0460-220-1231 / 0462-210-1231) for the 14 AWG feeds. You'd buy no size-20 contacts for the bulkhead: that's 33 pins + 33 sockets off the current order.
  • The injector and COP feeds stay on the A side (your Q-RAIL rule) in the A half's size-12 cavities. The DTP pass-through goes away.
  • Cost: about $146 for the housings and flange, plus 4 wedgelocks and cavity plugs.
  • Rule impact: one physical part, drawn as two logical halves: bh_a_* stays A-letter and bh_b_* stays B-letter. validate_bulkhead_letter.py L1–L7 keep working. Only the buy list needs to learn to count one DRB kit for the two halves.

Contact stock after the switch (my math)

  • Size-16 pins: 66 − 21 (old bulkhead 16s) + 54 = 99 of 130 owned.
  • Size-16 sockets: 62 − 21 + 54 = 95 of 118 owned.
  • Size-12: unchanged at 4 of 20. The 2 DTP feeds and 2 Lambda feeds just move into the DRB.
  • Size-20 to buy: pins 33 → 0, sockets 65 → 32. The remaining 32 sockets are for non-bulkhead connectors.

Tooling

  • The same Deutsch solid-contact crimper you use for size 16 and 12 (for example HDT-48-00).
  • No special removal tool: per the DRB instructions, you pull the wedgelock and release the finger with a small screwdriver.
  • Size-8 and size-4 contacts would need a heavier crimper, but only if you use them (optional below).

Suggested layout (to decide when drawing)

  • The 102 is built from two wedgelock halves (left and right), so an A half and a B half comes naturally.
  • A needs about 37 size-16 + 2 size-12 (injector, COP). B needs about 17 size-16 + 2 size-12 (Lambda).
  • The split won't be exactly 51/51, because A is bigger. Assign by cavity list from TE's cavity map, and keep the screens (A crank/cam, B knock) next to their signal pairs.

Optional add-on (not recommended yet)

The 4 size-8 (60 A) and 2 size-4 (100 A) positions could later carry the fan feeds (8 AWG; the fans are fused at 40/30 A) through this one hole instead of their current path. The engine-room doc says fans and EPS don't cross a signal bulkhead, so only do this if you decide to change that. EPS on a 60 A fuse sits right at a size-8 contact's rating, so keep it where it is.

Questions (only things not already settled)

  1. Do you accept one physical bulkhead drawn as two logical halves (A and B), with a small buy-list change? I recommend yes. It keeps every A/B validator rule as is and gets you to one firewall hole. If not, option 3 (DRB double flange) is the rule-clean fallback.
  2. DRB 102 or DRB 128? Same size shell. I recommend the 102: 33% spare size-16 plus 6 heavy-power positions for later. The 128 gives 52% spare size-16 but no power positions.
  3. Does a 126 × 75 mm flange fit where the two HDPs and the DTP would go? Please measure the firewall area, including engine-side room for the plug and jackscrew. If it's too tall, the DRC 70 (104 × 36 mm) plus the small DTP is the flat fallback.
