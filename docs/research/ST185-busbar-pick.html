Glove-box bus bar: 6 options

Needs from the harness files (pdb in EngineRoom-C + CabinPower + HeavyDC):
+ side: 6 studs = battery 1/0 in, RADLOK 1/0 out, LMI-B, MTA-1, MTA-2, MTA-3 (M8 lugs).
− side: 4 studs = battery 1/0 in, RADLOK 1/0, ID strap, IG strap.
Stud diagram: red = +, black = −. Big circle = M10, medium = M8, small = screw.

Honest finding: every Amazon / AliExpress "Positive & Negative" bar with 6+ studs per side is two separate bars sold as a pair, not one base. Mount the pair side by side on one plate and it takes about the same room. The true one-base bars I found (Victron, Pytes, and Daier's 250 A dual-row, quote only, 8×M10 total, 127×82 mm) have only about 4 studs per side, so the + side runs 2 short.
Ratings are what each seller claims; I tested none. Prices seen 2026-10-12.
