ST185 OEM blocks and part options

Part 2 only of the 2026-10-12 task (OEM block locations dropped per Dan). Nothing in the repo was changed. Web prices and stock are snapshots from 2026-10-12 and move. Every claim carries a source link or file path.

Short answers
  • MTA: 0301491 is real. MTA's 2019 catalog (p.22) lists it as a waterproof module with 6 MiniVal + 1 MaxiVal fuses, 2 Maxi relays, 1 Micro relay, exactly your need. No TE or Eaton part does the same. $20.29 each at Connector ID.
  • PDB: TE has no M8 stud block (its 250 A block is a screw-clamp DIN-rail part). Best real stud blocks: Victron VBB125040010 (4 studs) or Egis 6250-600 (6 studs). The placeholder says 8 studs, but the drawing uses only 3 cavities.
  • Relay: 4-1904124-2 is already the right number for V23074-A1001-A402 (resistor, 12 V, 1 Form A, 25 A). 5-1393292-8 is the diode version V23074-A2001-A402. TE datasheet p.4 settles it. No change needed.

2.2 Power distribution block (replaces placeholder "PDB-M8x8", 8 x M8 studs, 150 to 250 A)

Two facts about the placeholder. (1) The drawing calls it "Cabin PDB (glove box)", not engine bay: ST185-EngineRoom-C.harness connector pdb, and the power doc puts it in the passenger glove box. (2) That file uses only 3 cavities: c1 trunk battery + (1/0), c2 to RADLOK + (starter / 160 A alternator), c8 cabin ground bus. ST185-HeavyDC.harness was not checked for more. A 4 or 6 stud block may be enough; confirm the stud count before buying.

TE first: the only TE 250 A distribution block I found is ENTRELEC 1SNL325010R0000 (DBL250). It is DIN-rail with a screw clamp (6 mm Allen), not studs. Main wire range 2 AWG to 000 / 250 kcmil, so 1/0 fits the clamp, but there are no M8 studs for lugs. TE's page says it is not currently available.

2.3 Micro ISO relay drawn as 4-1904124-2

The drawing is right. Keep 4-1904124-2. TE datasheet V23074X0000A001 p.4 ("Micro Relay A"), 12 VDC rows:
TypeArrangementCoil suppressionTE PN
V23074-A1001-A4021 Form A, 1 NOResistor 680 Ω (NOR)4-1904124-2
V23074-A2001-A4021 Form A, 1 NODiode (NOD)5-1393292-8
V23074-A1001-A4031 Form C, 1 COResistor 680 Ω (COR)4-1904124-3
V23074-A2001-A4031 Form C, 1 CODiode (COD)6-1419137-4
"A402" only means 1 Form A. The middle code (A1001 vs A2001) decides resistor vs diode. The cross-reference that gave 5-1393292-8 for "A402" is the diode part. The datasheet also lists Production-in-Asia numbers for the same types (e.g. 1393292-5); ignore unless a distributor ships those.
