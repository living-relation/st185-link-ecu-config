3. The lesson to take from this — topology, not device list

Don't copy Dave's device list onto A-engine. Copy his branch style. Dave's "N681 TRD" loom is a different-generation reference (pre-COP, and it bundles alternator B+/sense and the A/C compressor clutch feed right into the engine loom). In this project those are deliberately split out:
  • Alternator B+ and charge sense, and the A/C clutch feed, live in Harness C (power/charging), not an ECU loom — see HARNESS-RECONCILE-PLAN.html §3 (A/C clutch already decided to move to Harness C this week).
  • Oil pressure, oil temp, fuel pressure, coolant pressure, VSS, and knock live on ST185-B-engine, not A-engine, per the ECU A/B connector-letter rule (docs/RECONCILIATION-RULES.md Rule 3).
  • A-engine's actual device set (per docs/harness/rebuild/ST185-A-engine.harness) is: crank, ect, iat, turbospd, cam, map, inj1-4, cop1-4 (×4, 1ZZ COP coils), boost, etb, flex, ac_cts (A/C amplifier's own coolant temp switch — not the ECU ECT), plus the bulkhead plug and the B-sensor-rail jumper to B-engine.
What's worth keeping from Dave's drawing is the pattern: one trunk running from the bulkhead down along the engine, with branch points at measured intervals, each branch point serving a tight physical cluster of nearby devices — rather than each device getting its own long individual run back to the connector. That's the "closer to what I'm going for" Dan asked for.

4. LHD mirroring — what changes, what doesn't

Dave's sketch is drawn for a right-hand-drive car, where the ECU/bulkhead connector sits on the passenger side of the firewall (the side away from the driver). On your left-hand-drive car, the driver sits on the left, so the DRB bulkhead is on the left side of the firewall instead — the mirror image, for the firewall-to-bulkhead portion only.

PortionChanges for LHD?Why
Firewall / bulkhead connector sideYes — mirrors left/rightDriver's footwell (and the ECU/bulkhead near it) is on the opposite side of the car for LHD vs RHD.
Trunk run from bulkhead toward the engineYes, direction flips to matchFollows from the bulkhead move — the first leg of the trunk now approaches the engine from the opposite side.
On-engine branch order, spacing, and clusteringNo — kept as drawnThe engine itself sits in the same physical location and orientation in the bay regardless of LHD/RHD; only the cabin side changes.
