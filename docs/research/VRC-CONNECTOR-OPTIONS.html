VRC connector options — one IN + one OUT per enclosure

Research note, 2026-10-03. Not committed. Feeds HARNESS-RECONCILE-PLAN.html §7 (decision 7). No CAD, BOM, or harness files touched.
PLANNING ONLY. Nothing ordered, nothing drawn. Every dimension marked “verify” must be checked against the maker's drawing before any panel is cut.
Rule change, 2026‑10‑04 (Dan, binding for every connector): “none of my connectors need to be shieldable. the drains just have to terminate to a pin.” Shieldability has been dropped as a criterion below. The ranking is now on temperature, sealing, size and pin count only.

1. What the connectors have to carry

Dan's call: exactly one input connector and one output connector per VRC box, front and rear. The count below comes from docs/devices/VR-WHEEL-SPEED-CONDITIONER.md (board needs +5V and sensor ground only; DIAG is held low on the board) and docs/enclosures/vr-conditioner-bom.csv.

ConnectorMust carryPins needed
INL VR+, L VR−, R VR+, R VR−, L drain, R drain (or one spliced drain, V1)5–6
OUT+5V, GND (sensor ground), OUT-L, OUT-R, output-cable drain(s) to the ECU5, or 6 if the front keeps two drains (FL → A7, FR → B17, plan decision 8)

So an 8-way connector covers both, with 2 spares each. Today's parts can't: IN-L/IN-R and OUT are 4-pin binder M8s (86 6618 1121 00004 / 86 6319 1121 00004), the OUT has no free pin for a drain, and they're rated only to 85 °C.

3. Recommendation

Re-rank after dropping shieldability: no change at the top. The shield requirement was never what favored DTM — temperature (125 °C), IP68 sealing, size and pin count were. Dropping it lets the unshielded Amphenol C16-3 8-pin onto the list, but it's bigger, only IP67, and a new contact family. The M8/M12 circulars still fail on temperature. A smaller DTM 6-way would fit exactly 6 pins with no spares and its flange drawing isn't verified — not recommended.
Option A: TE Deutsch DTM 8-way flange receptacles on the box, keyed A (grey) for IN and B (black) for OUT.
  • Meets every ask: 8 pins, IP68, −55 to +125 °C, drains on their own pins, and keying plus color so IN and OUT can't be swapped.
  • Deutsch connectors seal each wire on its own, so two cables can enter one plug. That keeps the front FL (A7) and FR (B17) output cables separate with their own drains (plan decision 8) while still using one OUT connector.
  • Size-20 contacts fit the 20 AWG cable cores. The house already uses Deutsch everywhere (bulkheads, inline pairs), so it's the same tools and crimpers.
  • Gender follows the house rule (DECISIONS D2): the side that supplies +5V gets sockets. The OUT cable plug (DTM06) has sockets, and the box gets pins on both connectors.

4. Things to check before this is final

  1. [Checked 2026‑10‑04 — see §5: does NOT fit; 1590WUFL recommended.] Fit in the Hammond 1590WYFL. From the Hammond drawing: 92 × 92 × 42 mm outside, inside depth 36.3–38 mm, lid opening 81.1 × 68.6 mm, side wall face about 79.5 mm wide by about 36 mm tall. A DTM 12-way flange is 35.6 mm tall, which is too tall for a side wall. Two flanges stacked on the lid (55.1 × 71.1 mm) fit the 92 × 92 lid, but the connector bodies reach about 40 mm into a 36–38 mm deep box, which may hit the board. This needs a CAD check (I didn't open CAD), plus the DTM 8-way flange drawing.
  2. [Checked — §5.3: M22759/16 cores pass, Spec 55 thin-wall fails.] Seal size. Thin-wall Tefzel 20 AWG cores may be under the 1.35 mm minimum insulation size for the DTM seal. Measure the cable cores you have on hand; if they're too thin, use build-up sleeves or a different cable.
  3. Gold or nickel contacts for 5 V signals. Check owned stock first. Standard DTM size-20 contacts are the 0460-202-20141 / 0462-201-20141 family — verify the PNs and plating before ordering.
  4. Gasket PN for the DTM 8-way flange. The 12-way uses DT12-L012-GKT; the 8-way equivalent hasn't been confirmed yet.
  5. [Resolved 2026‑10‑04: the +5V comes from ECU A32; the VRC doc gets fixed.] Side finding, not a connector issue: VR-WHEEL-SPEED-CONDITIONER.md says the board's 5 V comes from an external supply, not A32, but sot/channels.csv rows 82/93 and the drawings feed it from sp_5v (A32). One of them is stale. This goes on the plan as an open item.

5.4 Final verdict (2026‑10‑04)

DECIDED by Dan 2026‑10‑05: Hammond 1590WUFL, IN and OUT DTM 8-way on adjacent walls. The 1590Y-based VRC CAD in cad\enclosures\ is superseded.

The boards are tiny (openlogicefi dual VR conditioner), so the connectors drive the box size. Checked against TE’s 12-way flange drawing as the worst case (the 8-way drawing isn’t reachable: TE’s page is down and the DT panel-mount brochure lists no DTM flange parts).
  • 1590WYFL: no. 1.2 mm edge margin on a 38 mm face; 36.3 mm inside depth vs ~55 mm needed per connector.
  • 1590WUFL: yes. IN and OUT on two adjacent walls: 9.5 mm top/bottom margin, 32 mm side margin (worst-case 55.1 mm flange on the 120 mm wall), and a ~57 × 57 mm free corner for the board.
  • Gasket: TE’s neoprene flange gaskets are rated to 107 °C (TE DT panel-mount brochure). Fallback part if the DTM flange can’t be bought: DT04-08PA-CL03 (DT 8-way, E-seal, takes 1.35–3.05 mm insulation, same brochure).
