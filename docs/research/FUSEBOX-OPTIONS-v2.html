B · RFRM + 3 × MTA 0301491 + small LMI ✅

Pros
  • Relay + its fuse in one sealed module
  • Fewest parts to mount and wire
  • All in stock (Mouser, Connector ID)
  • Uses all 5 owned Maxi relays + 8 micro
Cons
  • ~$41 more than A
  • 3 small MTA modules + RFRM + LMI = 5 boxes
  • MTA covers / sealed terminals priced by estimate

A · RFRM + TE relay rail + LMI

Pros
  • Cheapest buy-now
  • Every rating published (TE, Eaton)
  • Uses the owned VCF7 socket too
  • Loose sockets = easy swaps
Cons
  • Most pieces: 6 loose sockets + 9-piece LMI
  • Most hand wiring between relays and fuses
  • Sockets not sealed (cabin OK)

MEC 97 + MTA 0301470 (quote sent separately)

Pros
  • One sealed box (IP69K) holds almost everything
  • Fewest housings
  • Clean OEM-style install
Cons
  • Quote only, price unknown
  • Custom pinout needed from MTA
  • 40 A fan/blower over MCP 2.8 pins (~19–22 A each) → MTA must parallel pins or use studs
  • LEAVYSEAL + RADLOK parts to buy

Single-unit search (wider vendors)

Result: no buy-now single housing holds 6 Maxi relays + 8 micro + ~30 minis + big fuses. Every off-the-shelf unit is either 2.8 mm (micro relay) only, or a controller with too few outputs. The closest single units are below. Each still needs a Maxi section.

S1 · Bussmann Large Fused Relay Center kit (Race Spec, $259, 8 in stock)

Pros
  • One box, kit includes terminals, seals and fuses
  • In stock, priced
  • Same RFRM as option A
Cons
  • Micro-relay slots only, so 40 A fan/blower can't go in
  • Still needs the Maxi relay rail + LMI → same as A
  • Kit relays duplicate the 8 TE relays you own
  • ≈ $94 more than A

S2 · Eaton mVEC 31M-300-0 (Connector Concepts, $469.80, 12 in stock)

Pros
  • Sealed IP66, 200 A, 30 A per pin (published)
  • Reports blown fuses/relays on CAN
  • In stock, priced
Cons
  • Relays switched by J1939 CAN, not Link low-side wires
  • 2.8 mm slots only → no Maxi relays
  • Still needs Maxi rail + LMI
  • Most expensive relay box

S3 · ECUMaster PMU16 (≈ $1,385–1,499)

Pros
  • Replaces all relays with smart outputs
  • Current logging, self-reset
  • Fewest relays/sockets
Cons
  • Short 1 output: 13 loads need 17 channels with pairing
  • Still needs a fuse box for unswitched circuits
  • Repo plan 6.48 dropped the PMU
  • Highest cost; often out of stock
Checked and ruled out
  • Eaton DVEC 32000: configurable, quote / surplus only (eBay 24 V $196.90). 2.8 mm only.
  • Bussmann RTMR 15305 / 15303: 10 fuses + 5 micro relays. Too small.
  • Eaton 15700 RTA: ATC fuses only, no relays.
  • Littelfuse HWB60 PDM21001LXM: 60 cav., 280 micro/mini relays. Waytek: discontinued.
  • MaxxECU Relay & Fuse box $190.82: 5 × 40 A relays, 6 fuses. Too small.
  • Haltech PD16 $1,099: works only with Haltech ECUs.
  • MoTeC PDM15 / AiM PDM08/32: $1,422–2,199, controller-type, same gaps as PMU16.
  • Hella 8JD/H84988001, Blue Sea, Painless/AAW/Ron Francis: fuse-only or 4-relay boxes. No unit near this size found.
  • Chief BRIC Maxi (C2571 $24.98): 30-pole single body. Too small.

Details

Circuit map (B)
  • 3 × 0301491 Maxi relays: k_efi, k_str, k_fan, k_fan2, heater, power (5 owned TE F7 + 1 bought). MaxiVal ×3: fan 40, heater 40, fan2 30. Their 18 MiniVal hold the 30 A circuits: POWER, AM2, RTR, k_efi, k_str.
  • RFRM: ~24 minis + 7 micro relays (k_etb, k_eps_en, k_acc, HEAD L/R, horn, k_fp), all owned TE V23074.
  • LMI AMI: AM1 40, EPS 60 → HCR150, OEM HOT 60 → J/B1, 1 spare.
Ratings checked (relay · terminal · pin · bus)
  • TE F7 Maxi: 70 A @23 °C / 50 A @85 °C. TE V23074 micro: 30 A / 25 A (local TE datasheets).
  • MP280 outputs (RFRM, MTA MiniVal): 2.8 mm terminals, 30 A max per terminal (Bussmann catalog).
  • MCP 2.8 (MEC LEAVYSEAL): ~19–22 A typical (TE listings) → too small alone for 40 A.
  • Bus sanity: RFRM 100 A per bus bar, 200 A max · LMI 400 A per assembly.
