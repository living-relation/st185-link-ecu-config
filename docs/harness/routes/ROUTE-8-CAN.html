Bus (settled, not reopened): one linear bus, 1 Mbit/s, 120 Ω at the two physical ends only — the ECU and the Link CAN-Lambda. Center cluster, CSB3 and the Pi USB-CAN adapter are mid-bus stubs, never terminated (CSB3 termination jumper OFF; PCAN/CANable termination OFF; the Waveshare hat is not on the bus). Keep each stub short (≤ ~0.3 m is the usual 1 Mbit/s guidance); with the trunk running past each device’s connector the stubs are a few cm. Node order along the trunk doesn’t affect the bus — only the two terminated ends matter, and those are unchanged.
