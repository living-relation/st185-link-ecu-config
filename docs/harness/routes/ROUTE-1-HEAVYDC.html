Text update (2026‑10‑13, text only; the figures and route lines are as Dan approved them, not redrawn): (1) The alternator IG / L / S plug wires leave through harness C’s RH grommet (PR #120), not through bulkhead B. IG detours along the RH sill to the Longacre 52-45780 kill switch’s 2nd pole in the trunk and back (PR #122, DECISIONS S21, R64). Where Figs 1–3 still show a short IG/L/S stub, read it as going to that grommet. (2) K1, confirmed by Dan in DECISIONS S20 (R63): the retract-motor feed and the motor ground both run 12 AWG to a crimp splice, then a 16 AWG pigtail (150 mm max) into the Toyota plug. These are not HeavyDC cables, so they are not in the table above.
