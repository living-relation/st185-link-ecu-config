VRC box (decided): Hammond 1590WUFL in the cabin, mid-dash right above the ECU. IN and OUT = two identical CNLINKO LP-16-J05PE-02-001 5-pin cable plugs (DECISIONS S8 / S16), mating two LP-16-C05SX-02-101 panel sockets on adjacent walls. The PCB is isolated from the case (nylon standoffs); the case is isolation-mounted; screens go by pin and join inside the box by wire, nothing bonds to the case (R31 / R33). Power: +5V from ECU A32 (sp_5v), ground from A24 Gnd Out (sp_gndout). Cabin mounting clears the engine-bay heat concern.

Drain choice (rev 3): one cable has one shield, so its drain can land on only one ECU shield pin. A7 is chosen because three of the four cores belong to connector A (+5V from A32, GND from A24, OUT-L to A23); the drain runs unbroken VRC OUT pin 5 → inline c5 → A-cabin → A7. After IX_WS_FRONT the FR signal leaves the shield for the short B-cabin lead to B21, which gets its own short shield to B17 (floating at the inline end), so the letter rule and one-drain-per-shield both hold; A7 and B17 never meet. Repo fix when applied: interfaces.json IX_WS_FRONT c6 → spare, front output cable → 4 cores; channels.csv VRC_FRONT_OUT screen note.
