Brake switch to CSB3 S7 (not the ECU)

ItemFindingSource
Switch level (EWD)12 V-active. 15A STOP fuse (J/B No.1 1B‑7, G‑R) → S9 pin 2 → closed to pin 1 with the pedal depressed → pin 1 (G‑W) carries +12 V to the stop lamps. Pedal released: pin 1 is open (floating, not grounded).TIS 1993 EWD p.126 (stop-light circuit) and p.127 (“S9 stop light SW, 1‑2: closed with brake pedal depressed”)
CSB3 input (manual)Ground-active. S1–S8 are “switch inputs, switched to ground”, “active when connected to ground”, with an internal 35 kΩ ±15 kΩ pull-up. The manual gives no 12 V rating for S inputs (only the analog inputs A1–A8 are “12 V tolerant”).ECUMaster CAN Switch Board V3 manual v2.1 (FW 3.0+), p.3 specification, p.4 connection, p.5 pin table
VerdictThe CSB3 cannot take the S9 signal as-is. It is the wrong polarity (+12 V when pressed, floating when released), and 12 V on an S input is outside what the manual allows. An optoisolator is needed: LED side across the brake-lamp feed (S9 pin 1, G‑W) to body ground; transistor side — collector to S7 (c21), emitter to SGND (c4). Pedal pressed → S7 pulled to ground = active.derived from the two rows above
Where the ECU gets itCSB3 → CAN 0x642 byte 4 SW_MASK, S7 = bit 6 (S1 = bit 0) → Link ECU over CAN bus 1 (.lcs receive 0x642 “Brake Switch” → VDI7). No ECU connector or pin. sot/channels.csv has no brake row (no ECU pin exists for it).manual p.11; FL_BRAKE_SW_C1 in interfaces.json; Route 12 key (S7 = c21)
