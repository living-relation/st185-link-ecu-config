plan 6.41: loom boundaries - what belongs in A, B and C

Settled 2026-09-22, after pass 2 turned up ECU-A wires riding bulkhead B, EPS
wires riding bulkhead B, and the CSB3 owned by three drawings at once.

Looms A and B are independent. The ECU pin decides which loom a wire joins.
Anything not bound for its own bulkhead branches at the ECU connector and runs
direct to its loom. Looms A and B carry only wires entering the engine bay
through their own bulkhead, and nothing crosses from A or B into another loom on
the engine-bay side. Cabin-side branching is fine - the reverse switch comes in
on A or B and branches under the dash to the CSB3.

EPS is loom C only, ECU trigger included.

The CSB3, the cluster LED loom and the dash devices get their own cabin
accessory drawing, and the HD30 connector is owned there once.

Wheel speed sensor runs move to loom C. The conditioner outputs merge into
whichever loom holds their ECU pin - FL to loom A, FR/RL/RR to loom B. The boxes
are in the cabin so those outputs never cross a bulkhead.

Also records the six known violations to fix and two open items: where the rear
wheel sensors ride, and the stale harness file list in RECONCILIATION-RULES.md.
