SoT: sot/channels.csv is now the root, and check_all gates on it

Every ECU A/B/comms pin plus the VRC, A/C amplifier, EPS relay and MRS pump
channels live in one machine-readable file with owner, class, pull-up, shield
pin, status and source. validate_sot.py checks the SoT is self-consistent and
then checks every drawing against it - 63 ECU landings, zero disagreements.

Two things the evidence settled along the way:
  - B12 / Ign 6 is NOT available. It is the EPS pump relay trigger, already
    drawn in EngineRoom-C (k_eps coil 85). The IO table said "Available".
  - The A/C pressure switch is pin 1 and pin 4 per EWD p152, not c1/c2, and
    the ambient sensor return is a shared sensor common whose amplifier pin
    is on neither p150 nor p152 - left TBD rather than invented.
