6.48 rewritten: no PDU, no added logic - and loom C laid out properly

Daniel cut the scope, correctly. The first draft of 6.48 invented a shutdown
system nobody asked for. Rewritten in place rather than stacked behind another
supersede banner, because the supersede chain was already three deep.

Withdrawn entirely: the device-hold relay, the PDU-controlled battery kill, the
alternator excite sequencing, and the permanently live CSB3.

  - CSB3, cluster and Pi each get a fuse off ignition-switched 12 V. They come up
    with the key and die with it. The CSB3 is not always live; that idea only ever
    existed to serve a kill sequence that no longer exists.
  - The Pi is an info display. It has no role in any shutdown.
  - Battery kill is a manual master cutoff in the trunk. No relay, no ECU, no
    sequencing. The HCR150 goes back to being just the EPS pump relay.
  - The only logic on the car is the ECU's own hold power on Aux 6, exactly as
    already built and entirely self-contained.

All four CSB3 low-sides are free again; the oil lamp is the only candidate.
Relay count drops to 3-4 and the second fuse block to 12 ways.

6.43 and 6.45 now carry withdrawal banners rather than being left live. Two
findings from 6.45 are carried forward into 6.48 because they still matter: a
130 A relay was never going to carry 100-300 A of cranking, and opening a master
switch on a running alternator is a load dump, so buy a switch with an excite-kill
terminal.

Corrected an overstatement of mine: I wrote that nothing in the cabin changes.
Wrong. The dash branch wiring is untouched, but power and ground delivery to the
junction blocks is entirely new - seven new terminations, from a PDB that did not
exist, on a battery that now lives in the trunk. 6.48 lists them.

Layout. Loom C had 38 nodes spread over 5500 x 3300 with all four section headers
stacked at x=0, nowhere near the zones they name. Now four zones in reading order -
trunk and glove box, firewall RADLOK, OEM injection, new engine-room loads - each
under its own header, with cross-reference stubs in a fifth column. Canvas is
3150 x 2220. layout_room_c.py holds the zone table so it can be re-run.

Harness node updated to match: the ex-pmu body block's CSB3 and device cavities
are plain switched feeds now.
