plan 6.41: CSB3 keeps the 6.40 connector, trunk loom joins on an inline DT

Two decisions settled 2026-09-22.

CSB3 connector: the 6.40 pair stands - HD34-24-33PE receptacle, HD36-24-33SE
plug, 26 size-20 sockets bought new. The 21-way HD30 pair already on the shelf
was the cheaper road and lost on merit: it puts pins on the harness so the live
12 V sits unshrouded when unplugged, and 21 ways cannot bring out all 26 CSB3
terminals, which is the entire point of 6.37. That pair stays free for something
else. Size-20 contacts are now a confirmed TE buy - none on hand, and DigiKey had
the socket at zero on 2026-09-21.

Rear trunk loom junction: one inline connector, not a bulkhead. Nine conductors
cross it - two conditioned rear wheel pulses, the VRC 5 V and ground, the output
cable screen, fuel level signal and ground, and the pump pair. Forward of the
connector they split by ECU pin into loom A or B as 6.41 requires.

The connector comes off the shelf per the new sourcing rule: DT04-12PA /
DT06-12SA with W12P / W12S wedgelocks, a sealed 12-way Deutsch DT pair, two sets
owned and unassigned. Twelve ways covers nine with three spare, and DT takes the
size 16 contacts already in stock - the 16-20 AWG variant for the signals, the
14 AWG variant for the pump pair. Marked CLAIMED in the on-hand CSV.

Open, and worth measuring before anyone crimps: the fuel pump's running current.
A size 16 contact is good for about 13 A, so a big pump plus trunk-length voltage
drop is the one thing that would push the pump pair into its own connector.
