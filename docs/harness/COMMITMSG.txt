plan 6.48: no PDU - relays, a second fuse block, and the CSB3 does the logic

Daniel's decision. The PMU-16 is not being bought. This closes 6.46 and rewrites
every PMU assumption in the repo.

What replaces it. Both junction blocks stay. The five ex-J/B2 circuits get relays
and a second fuse block in the glove box - HEAD LH and HEAD RH and RTR on relays,
HAZ-HORN and DOME on fuses alone. AM1 and AM2 go back to plain fused PDB feeds,
which is what redistribution 3.1 always said before 6.44 briefly moved them. So
the net effect of the entire PDU question is that nothing in the cabin changes.

The logic moves to the CSB3's four low-sides: L1 device relay hold for the Pi's
clean shutdown, L2 kill relay coil, L3 alternator excite, L4 oil lamp. Full, none
spare. The CSB3 itself takes a permanent fused feed because it has to be alive
through the kill sequence and cannot hold the relay that powers it - parasitic
draw flagged for measurement.

Shutdown order is unchanged in intent: ECU parks the ETB on its own hold power and
is the innermost authority, the Pi shuts down on CAN state, the CSB3 releases the
device relay when the Pi reports down, and only an actual kill command drops
excitation and then the HCR150. Normal key-off is not a kill.

Docs updated. Supersede banners on 6.38, 6.44 and 6.45 saying exactly which parts
survive - the DTM4 house standard and load figures in 6.38 still stand, and so
does 6.44's injection-point constraint, which is the reason both J/Bs stay
whether or not a PDU exists. Redistribution 3.1 gets a translation table so its
thirty "PMU Ox" references stay readable instead of being hand-edited into
inconsistency; the architecture diagram, fuse schedule, build steps and power-up
order are corrected properly.

Harness updated too, rather than left to drift. Loom C's pmu node is now "Glove-box
body block (fuses + relays, ex-J/B2)" - same element id so its seven wires survive,
same cavity count, new label and cavity text, and the two dead CAN cavities
repurposed as the CSB3 permanent feed and the device relay feed. Live on
harness.design and mirrored locally by fix_no_pdu.py.

Buy list swaps the PMU-16 for 4 micro ISO relays, a 12-16 way fuse block and a
100 A ANL. Lint 8/8, verify clean at 269 conductors, both lists regenerated.
