CSB3 on the real HD30 connector, one copy, plus the cruise-ladder short

The CSB3 existed three times with three different cavity maps - a 14-way on
A-ECU, a 2-way on B-ECU, a 14-way on CAN - and none of them matched the part
we are actually buying. Now there is one connector part, HD36-24-33SE, built
to the 6.37 cavity map that 6.40 re-homed onto HD30:

  1-2   power        5-6   CAN H/L      7-14  A1-A8
  15-22 S1-S8        23-26 L1-L4        27-33 spare, plug 0413-204-2005

Contacts are 0462-201-2031 size 20 sockets, the same family as bulkhead A, so
the box stays on one crimp tool. Matches docs/enclosures/csb3-bom.csv.

A-ECU carries the connector. B-ECU and CAN show the same cavities with no part
assigned, so the plug is counted once in the BOM, not three times.

Also fixed: the cruise ladder had the stalk signal AND the pull-up resistor
landing on one cavity, which is a short. They meet at sp_cruise_ladder now and
one wire runs to A2.
