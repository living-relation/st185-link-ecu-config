Every circuit crosses on the bulkhead its ECU pin belongs to

Knock 1 is ECU B9 but all three of its conductors crossed on bulkhead A - SIG+
on c27, SIG- on c6, screen on c35. The screen was the tell: it crossed bulkhead
A and then terminated on SHIELD_B at the ECU, taking the long way round its own
loom. Moved to bh_b c16 / c17 / c18, both halves.

Aux 7 MRS speed-out is the mirror image - ECU pin A27, so a loom-A circuit, but
it crossed on bh_b c13. Moved to bh_a c36, and the EngineRoom-C dummy renamed.

Deleted w51_c and w54_c. These were the cabin halves of the front wheel-speed
runs from before the NCV1124 conditioner went in. Their engine halves were
retired into cab_fout_l / cab_fout_r at the time; the cabin halves were not, so
A23 and B21 each had TWO conductors on them - the conditioner's clean output
AND a bulkhead cavity whose other half went nowhere. Two outputs on one input.
validate_sot.py grew rule D4 to catch exactly this, with an exemption for a
conductor that runs to a resistor or diode, because the cam Hall pull-up and
the CAN terminators share an ECU pin legitimately.

Canonicalised six bulkhead cavity names. The cabin copy said "terminates at the
ECU" and the engine copy said "floats at the sensor" for the same screen pin -
both true, and neither belongs in a cavity name. That behaviour lives in
SHIELD-RULES.md and sot/channels.csv; the cavity gets the plain circuit name.

audit_pin_names.py is now a HARD gate. It reported zero for the first time
today and both of Daniel's absolute rules live in it.

  shield-rule violations      0
  pin-name violations        11 -> 0
  one-sided bulkhead cavities 7 -> 2
  ECU signal pins with two sources  2 -> 0
