Apply 6.33 layout standard to all eight rebuild files

layout_633.py places every connector, splice and ring terminal into the
column band 6.33 calls for - rear of car, cabin, ECU A next to ECU B left
of centre, the two cabin bulkheads, the two engine bulkheads facing them,
then engine bay and front of car on the right. Driver-side items sit high
in each column, passenger-side low.

Also in the same pass, because all three broke the drawings:

- Ring terminals were never being placed at all. They now get zones.
- Wire layoutPoints were hand-placed against the old coordinates, so every
  one of them was an elbow pointing at empty space. Dropped; the app routes.
- Schematic notes were anchored to the old grid and landed on top of
  connectors. Stacked into a banner above the drawing.
- Labels that went through cp1252 came back as mojibake. Repaired, and the
  scripts now open files with an explicit utf-8 encoding so it stops
  happening.
- width is a strict enum in v0.9 - 60/90/150/210/270/390 only. 240, 300 and
  540 were in the files and are a hard upload reject. snap_widths() rounds
  up to the next legal step.
