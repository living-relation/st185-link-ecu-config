harness: one folder per generation, and fix the two stale generators

Daniel could not tell which harness file was current. Fair - there were 24
.harness files for 8 real looms, in four places, with no map.

Sorted into generations:

  rebuild/          the 8 current looms - the source, matches harness.design
  min/              build product of rebuild/, upload copies
  legacy-prebuild/  the 4 pre-split looms, frozen (was docs/harness/*.harness)
  archive/cursor-engineroom-experiments/  Cursor's throwaway variants

docs/harness/README.md is the new map: what each folder is, which of the eight
looms does what, the pipeline, and the rule that a new generation gets a new
folder rather than a -v2 suffix.

legacy-prebuild/ cannot be deleted yet. verify_rebuild.py diffs every conductor
in rebuild/ against it, and that diff is the only thing standing between us and a
silently dropped wire. It goes when 6.41 lands and a new baseline is taken.

The real find: buylist.py and buildlist.py were both still reading the pre-split
files. Every buy list this project has generated was built from the wrong
harnesses. Repointed at rebuild/, and the first run immediately surfaced what was
hidden - 68 size-20 sockets and 42 size-20 pins needed, zero on hand, plus the
HD36-24-33SE. That is the same gap the TE BOM turned up yesterday, which nobody
would have caught from NEED-TO-BUY.md.

Both generators now read rebuild/ and write back up to docs/harness/, and
verify_rebuild.py reads its baseline from legacy-prebuild/. Pipeline re-run:
lint 8/8 OK, verify MATCH at 269 conductors, both lists regenerated.

Also put a banner at the top of the consolidation plan. Sections 1-5 of that
document describe the two-file Power/Signal world of 2026-09-12 and flatly
contradict 6.41, which is most of why the repo felt unreadable. The banner states
the precedence: 6.x beats 1-5, highest subsection wins, and 6.41 defines the looms.
