plan 6.47: surveyed bigger PDUs for option C - none of them work here

Daniel asked whether a different PDU has the channels and current to make
deleting both junction blocks worth it. Priced and spec'd the field today.

Option C needs about 24 outputs with three of them carrying 30-40 A.

  ECUMaster PMU-16   16 ch, 25 A max    $1,499   too few
  Haltech PD16       16 ch, 25 A max    $1,099   DISQUALIFIED - Haltech Elite or
                                                 Nexus ECU only, will not run
                                                 standalone, and we are on a Link
  Racepak SmartWire  ~30 ch             $1,750   closest fit
  MoTeC PDM32        32 ch, 20 A max    $5,165   absurd here, and no channel
                                                 even reaches 25 A
  2 x PMU-16         32 ch              $2,998   works, silly

Two things kill option C whichever one is bought.

The heater blower is 40 A and the best channel in the field is 25 A, so the
blower keeps a relay and a fuse regardless - which means R/B No.4 cannot actually
be deleted, and DEFOGGER and RTR both need paralleled channels everywhere.

And J/B No.1's integration relay runs intermittent wipers, courtesy delay and the
seatbelt and key warnings. Replacing it means running door, seatbelt and key
switches into the PDM as inputs and writing the logic - more wires into the dash,
which is the opposite of what Daniel asked for.

So: no. 6.46's recommendation stands and this strengthens it.

Added the cheap way to get the one thing a PDU was genuinely offering. The CSB3
has eight analog inputs and only two are used. Hall current sensors on the six
circuits worth watching feed A3-A8, go out on CAN, and display on RealDash and the
TrackCluster, both already on the bus. About $60, no new module, no new CAN node.
