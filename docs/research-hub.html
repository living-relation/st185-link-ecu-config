I4 · PDF export: notes on the small harness drawings

R88 wants every note inside the frame of the drawing's parts. On small drawings like WheelSpeed-Front/-Rear (1530 x 780) the 5 approved notes are about 890 tall, so they don't fit without covering wires or moving parts, and moving parts would change the approved routing (R90). RearLights has the same problem. Until you pick an option, these drawings ship with bands, labels and the title block, but without the PDF export setup.

  • A Allow one notes column right beside the drawing (page size Fit makes the sheet big enough for it); change the gate to match recommended
  • B Spread the parts out to make room inside the drawing (re-route; you approve the drawings again)
  • C Cut the notes down until they fit in the gaps
I1 · PDF export: notes on the compact AntiTheft and RearLights drawings

The PDF export rule (R88) wants every note inside the drawing's frame. AntiTheft and RearLights are too tight: their notes only fit beside the drawing (AntiTheft's cavity-notes list alone is taller than the whole drawing). Both have their bands, title block and band legend; only the export setup waits. HeavyDC and RearFuel are done.

  • A Spread the parts a little to make room (layout only - same wires, pins and parts; wire paths re-routed) recommended
  • B Shorten the notes so they fit
  • C Let notes sit beside the drawing (change the export check)
