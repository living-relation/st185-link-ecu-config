ST185 Cluster LED Diagram crown

1991 Celica GT-Four ST185 · residual dash stubs + Link/CSB · living face on pass-a-bh-c-delete-locks

A) Residual LEDs

High beam

C11-12 · R-L

Body residual high-beam feed. LED on when high-beam circuit is hot.

Turn L

C11-2 · G-B

Flasher LH stub. Blink with left turn / hazard.

Turn R

C11-11 · G-Y

Flasher RH stub. Blink with right turn / hazard.

Park

C12-1 · R-G

Park-brake warning. Grounds via P1 / B2 fluid path on OEM side.

Charge

C12-9 B-O (IG) + C12-8 Y (alt L)

Series path: IG feed → charge LED → alternator L. LED ON when regulator grounds L (not charging); OFF when L is near battery (charging).

Oil

CSB · 0x3F1 bit4 → 0x643 LS

Link sets Low Oil Press 2 on 0x3F1 bit4; PCLink packs CSB low-side on 0x643. CSB LS sinks LED cathode (anode to +12 IG). Active-low / GND switch.

Charge LED path: IG (+12 switched) ── C12-9 B-O ── [12V charge LED] ── C12-8 Y ── alt L Oil LED path: Link 0x3F1 bit4 ── CSB 0x643 LS ── sinks LED cathode (anode = +12 IG)

B) Alternator terminals (not LED faces)

OEM charging regulator terminals. These are wiring/power nodes — only L drives the charge LED face above. S is sense, not a lamp.

B
B+ output — heavy DC to starter B+ / charge cable (Power harness).
S
Battery sense / voltage feedback to regulator. NOT a lamp.
IG
Ignition-switched +12 into regulator (feed side of charge LED path via C12-9).
L
Charge-lamp drive — C12-8 Y to custom cluster LED (section A).
