BELT INSTALL — ORDER OF OPERATIONS
# Step Detail
1 Oil pump pulley SST 09616-30011 → 35 N·m
2 Crank timing pulley Key aligned. Flange side INWARD.
3 No.2 idler 43 N·m. Spins free.
4 No.1 idler Loctite 242 on threads → 52 N·m. Bracket moves free.
5 Crank key groove UP Then route belt: crank → oil pump → No.2 idler → water pump → No.1 idler
6 Belt guide Cup side OUTWARD.
7 No.1 cover → crank pulley 6 bolts → pulley bolt 108 N·m
8 Cam sprockets Cam groove to drilled mark on No.1 bearing cap. Pulley "S" mark UP. Knock pin in. 59 N·m (41 with SST).
9 No.1 to TDC/compression Crank groove → "0" on No.1 cover. Both cam marks → No.3 cover.
10 Belt onto cam sprockets Keep the crank→intake cam run TIGHT. Drive all slack to the tensioner side.
DANGER ENGINE MUST BE COLD. ALWAYS TURN THE CRANK CLOCKWISE ONLY. Backward rotation lets the belt jump on the slack
side.
TENSIONER — HYDRAULIC, PINNED. THIS IS NOT A SPRING TENSIONER.
Step Detail
A Press push rod in 100–1,000 kg (220–2,205 lb). Pin with 1.27 mm hex key. Release press.
B Preload No.1 idler Turn idler bolt CCW to 18 N·m, as far left as it goes. Fit tensioner, 2 bolts loose.
C Crank 5/6 turn CW Groove → ATDC 60° mark on No.1 cover.
D Set the gap 1.90 mm feeler between tensioner body and No.1 idler stopper. Idler bolt CCW to 18 N·m. Push tensioner, tighten 2
bolts alternately to 21 N·m. Pull the 1.27 mm pin.
E Crank 1 turn CW Groove → ATDC 60° again. Idler bolt CCW to 18 N·m.

THE MATH — MEMORISE THESE FOUR LINES
Rule
Crank vs cam 1 cam degree = 2 crank degrees. The wheel reads crank. A 6° wheel error = 3° at the gear.
Centerline by
averaging Record wheel angle at 1.00 mm before peak and 1.00 mm after peak. Centerline = average of the two.
Centerline from
events IN: (duration ÷ 2) − open BTDC | EX: (duration ÷ 2) − close ATDC
Self-check IN (227÷2)−4 = 109.5 ≈ 110 ✓ EX (226÷2)−10 = 103 ✓ Both methods must agree within 1°.
TRUE TDC — DO THIS FIRST, EVERY TIME
Step
1 Wheel on crank snout with spacers. Stiff pointer on a block bolt. All 4 plugs out.
2 No.1 near TDC → rotate ~15° backward → install 14 mm screw-in piston stop.
3 Rotate forward to contact — record. Rotate backward to contact — record.
4
True TDC = exact midpoint. Zero the wheel there. Run it twice before you believe it. Cast pulley mark and ATI etched mark may read 3–5°
out — that is normal, trust the wheel. Scribe your zero on the damper.
CORRECTION DIRECTION — TURBO 3S
Measured Meaning Effect
IN c/line < 110° ADVANCED More low-end torque, quicker spool, earlier boost. Costs top end.
IN c/line > 110° RETARDED More top end. Softer bottom, later spool.
EX c/line > 103° ADVANCED More overlap. Usually hurts a turbo motor.
EX c/line < 103° RETARDED Wider effective LSA, less overlap. Adds top end, little low-end cost.
NOTE Start at the card: 110 / 103. ATS range is ±10 cam deg (±20 crank). Sweep on a dyno in 2° steps — do not guess at the wheel.
Community starting points conflict (0/−6 vs +4/−8); they are starting points, not specifications. Re-torque set screws after the first
heat cycle.
BEFORE FIRST START — NON-NEGOTIABLE
DANGER THIS ENGINE IS INTERFERENCE. 5S-FE stroke + HKS 264 lift removes the stock 3S-GTE margin. Check valve-to-piston with
clay or a dial indicator, with the FINAL springs fitted, by hand, through two full revolutions. Re-check after ANY change to cam
timing. Never crank on the starter until verified.
