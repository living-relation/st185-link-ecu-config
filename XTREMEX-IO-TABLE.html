Drive types: Aux 1–4 = low-side only. Aux 5–10 = high or low side per Link — here Aux 9–10 are dedicated to the ETB H-bridge. Spare Ign/Inj = low-side. Low-side outputs sink to ground when active and float (hi-Z) when off — no forced high, no pull-up unless a device needs one. Active-HIGH loads must use Aux 5–8 (or a relay), since Aux 9–10 are committed to the ETB.

Wire scheme: one solid colour per signal group — Red 12V · Black ECU ground · Black/White sensor ground (Gnd Out) · Green/Yellow shield drain · Orange +5V · Orange/White +8V · Red/White V-Ethrottle · White analog · Yellow temp · Blue trigger · Green digital · Grey knock · Brown injector · Violet ignition · Pink aux. Individual channels are identified by a printed pin-number marker at both ends, not by stripe colour — ten spools instead of forty, and the pin number is readable years later. Pin numbers verified against the G4X XtremeX Quickstart Guide loom diagrams, cross-checked against Link dealer wire-gauge data (A5/B5 red 0.75mm², A25/A34/B25/B34 black) — 2026-09-06.
✔ Confirmed hardware
  • Crank trigger: Toyota OEM reluctor (VR) on 36-2 crank wheel (Beams/5S) → Trigger 1.
  • Cam trigger: RacerX MR2 kit, Cherry Hall, intake cam, single tooth → Trigger 2.
  • Ignition: 1ZZ coil-on-plug (built-in igniter, logic-level) → Ign 1–4. No distributor.
  • Injectors: 1400cc ATS/Bosch top-feed, flow-matched (hi-Z → saturated) → Inj 1–4.
  • Throttle: Bosch Motorsport 74.5mm ETB → motor Aux 9/10, TP main+sub + APS main+sub on An Volt, power via V-Ethrottle (relay Aux 2).
  • Flex sensor: Continental 3-pin (freq) → ethanol % and fuel temp (no separate fuel-temp sensor).
  • Starter: ECU-controlled — start input + start-relay output; ECU disables the start relay in software under set conditions (no separate cut relay). EFI main relay held by ECU for DBW key-off reset.
⚠ Output power routing
  • Switchboard outputs = 4× low-side @ 0.5A (LED level) only. Use it for switch/analog inputs over CAN + tiny LEDs — never fans/pumps/relays.
  • All power loads on the ECU (Aux + spare Ign/Inj → external relays, active-low). Overflow → ECUMaster PMU/PDM on CAN 1.
  • Switch inputs → switchboard (brake, reverse, cruise) to keep ECU DIs for frequency inputs.

AC control (per your AC-amp notes)

ECU does not drive the compressor. The only AC signal on the ECU is this kill output. Aux 4 is a low-side output feeding the amplifier's ACT cancel input — active-low, floats when inactive, no pull-up. Used to cancel the compressor under high load/throttle/boost. No AC-on input — the XtremeX closed-loop idle absorbs the AC-load dip in software. Polarity resolved: Aux 4 drives the amplifier's ACT terminal — ground = kill, floating = normal. Active-low sink, no pull-up. AC1 is an input to the amplifier and must never be used as a kill signal.

External pull-up resistors

None required in the current plan. The flex sensor supplies fuel temp, which freed a Temp input, so every thermistor (ECT, IAT, oil, charge-pipe IAT) uses the ECU's built-in pull-ups. The MRS speed-out and AC-kill are low-side pulse/sink outputs that need no resistor. One exception: the cam Hall on Trigger 2 takes a 1.8k pull-up to +8V Out. The kit ships a 2.4k, which is sized for a 12V supply — do not fit it on the 8V rail. Confirmed no benefit to running the kit's intended 12V instead: the sensor's own datasheet (Cherry/Bluegrass-family Hall sensor, sold by RacerX) rates it 5–24V, open-collector output, with pull-up values given at both supplies — 1.8k at 9V, 2.4k at 12V — which is exactly where our 1.8k/8V and the kit's 2.4k/12V come from. Output level and current draw do not change with supply voltage in that range. Staying on the ECU's own +8V Out keeps the sensor on a dedicated regulated supply instead of tapping the switched 12–14V rail for one more load.

Base-map logic to implement (from your notes)

Idle-up via closed-loop idle (absorbs AC load, no AC input); inverse-VSS idle-up for EPS current draw; AC-kill by load/throttle/boost; heat-soak comp from dual IAT (manifold Temp 2 + charge-pipe Temp 4); flat-shift; traction from wheel speeds (AWD — front-axle vs rear-axle slip); reverse switch → PCLink Trigger sets Gear = 7 (R) on 0x3EB so the cluster shows "R" (and RealDash's reverse-camera view). (Tune functions, not pins.)

Harness-build tasks

  1. Loom B +5V jumper. Loom B has no native +5V pin. Coolant pressure, fuel level and turbo speed need 5V jumpered across from the Loom A +5V Out. Build this into the harness — it is not an ECU setting. (Turbo speed sits on DI 1 in the A loom, so confirm whether its 5V is already local before running a jumper.)
  2. Cam Hall pull-up. Fit 1.8k from signal to +8V Out. Do not fit the 2.4k in the RacerX kit — that value assumes a 12V supply.
  3. Shields. Terminate every shield at the ECU end only, floating at the sensor end. Shields carry no current.
  4. Sensor grounds. Gnd Out only, never chassis. APS and TPS may share a Gnd Out.
