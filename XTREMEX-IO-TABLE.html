Drive types (QSG + PCLink-family wiring manual): Aux 1–4 = flywheeled, low-side only, PWM / VVT / GP (2 A). Aux 5–8 = flywheeled high or low side, PWM / VVT / GP / stepper (low 2 A; high-side stepper 0.5 A). Aux 9 / 10 = E-throttle motor +/− or GP push-pull (4 A) — committed to the Bosch ETB H-bridge, not generic high/low aux. Spare Ign = 5 V / 20 mA logic high, or 2 A low-side aux without a flywheel diode. Spare Inj = high-Z saturated drive, or 2 A low-side aux without a flywheel diode. Low-side outputs sink to ground when active and are hi-Z when off. Active-HIGH loads must use Aux 5–8 (or a relay).

Wire scheme (this harness): one solid colour per signal group — Red 12V · Black ECU ground · Black/White sensor ground (Gnd Out) · Green/Yellow shield drain · Orange +5V · Orange/White +8V · Red/White V-Ethrottle · White analog · Yellow temp · Blue trigger · Green digital · Grey knock · Brown injector · Violet ignition · Pink aux. Channels are identified by a printed pin-number marker at both ends. Factory Link loom stripe colours (QSG) are in the colour table below — do not mix the two schemes on one end. Power pins A5 / B5 = 0.75 mm²; ECU grounds A25/A34/B25/B34 = black.
✔ Confirmed hardware
  • Crank trigger: Toyota OEM reluctor (VR) on 36-2 crank wheel (Beams/5S) → Trigger 1.
  • Cam trigger: RacerX MR2 kit, Cherry Hall, intake cam, single tooth → Trigger 2.
  • Ignition: 1ZZ coil-on-plug (built-in igniter, logic-level) → Ign 1–4. No distributor.
  • Injectors: 1400cc ATS/Bosch top-feed, flow-matched (hi-Z → saturated) → Inj 1–4.
  • Throttle: Bosch Motorsport 74.5mm ETB → motor Aux 9/10, TP main+sub. APS is a Subaru BRZ 6-pin pedal (VPA1→An Volt 4 A14, VPA2→An Volt 5 B33), power via V-Ethrottle (relay Aux 2).
  • Flex sensor: Continental 3-pin (freq) → ethanol % and fuel temp (no separate fuel-temp sensor).
  • Starter: ECU-controlled — start input + start-relay output; ECU disables the start relay in software under set conditions (no separate cut relay). EFI main relay held by ECU for DBW key-off reset.
⚠ Output power routing
  • Switchboard outputs = 4× low-side @ 0.5A (LED level) only. Use it for switch/analog inputs over CAN + tiny LEDs — never fans/pumps/relays.
  • All power loads on the ECU (Aux + spare Ign/Inj → external relays, active-low). Overflow → ECUMaster PMU/PDM on CAN 1.
  • Switch inputs → switchboard (brake, reverse, cruise) to keep ECU DIs for frequency inputs.

AC control (per your AC-amp notes)

ECU does not drive the compressor. The only AC signal on the ECU is this kill output. Aux 4 is a low-side output feeding the amplifier's ACT cancel input — active-low, floats when inactive, no pull-up. Used to cancel the compressor under high load/throttle/boost. No AC-on input — the XtremeX closed-loop idle absorbs the AC-load dip in software. Polarity resolved: Aux 4 drives the amplifier's ACT terminal — ground = kill, floating = normal. Active-low sink, no pull-up. There is no AC1 wire in this install; ACT is the only AC signal.

Pull-ups and terminal capabilities

Confirmed against the XtremeX Quick Start Guide (2023-06-22) and the PCLink-family wiring manual.
  • An Temp 1 / 2 (A15 / A16): selectable internal pull-up, or none. Set both to 1 kΩ internal so they match Temp 3/4. No external resistor on ECT or manifold IAT.
  • An Temp 3 / 4 (B6 / B7): fixed 1 kΩ to 5 V (QSG). Oil temp and charge-pipe IAT stay on these pins; same NTC as Temp 2 still needs its own PCLink cal table.
  • An Volt 1–11: no internal pull-up, 0–5 V only. Fuel level on An Volt 9 therefore does need an external divider to +5 V (value TBD at calibration — 470 Ω is a starting guess for a low-ohm sender, not a confirmed part).
  • Digital inputs: software-selectable pull-up, active state, all channels to 10 kHz (product spec). Enable the DI pull-up for the start switch (to GND) and for the Continental flex sensor on DI 2 (Ethanol Sensor function). Do not add a second 2.4 kΩ on the flex signal unless a scope shows a weak edge — the ECU pull-up is the intended source.
  • Trigger 2 cam Hall: open-collector. Fit 1.8 kΩ to +8 V Out (A6). Do not fit the kit's 2.4 kΩ (that value assumes 12 V). Sensor is 5–24 V; staying on the ECU +8 V rail is correct.
  • Aux 4 AC kill: flywheeled low-side. With no inductive load the pin is hi-Z when off — no pull-up. Amp ACT: ground = kill, floating = normal.
  • Aux 7 MRS SPD: see the Aux 7 row — confirm high level on a scope before adding a 5 V pull-up.
  • Connectors: two 34-way AMP Superseal 1.0 (Link Connector A + B kits, gold terminals). TE 3-1447221-4 = 0.5 mm² / 20 AWG; 3-1447221-3 = 0.75–1.25 mm² / 18–16 AWG for A5 / B5 / grounds. Housing rating ~15 A; the ECU pin is the limit, not the terminal.
  • B14: empty cavity on the official B-loom drawing — no terminal, no wire.
  • Loom B has no +5 V pin — jumper from A32 (+5 V Out) for An Volt 6/7/8/9 (and any other B-loom 5 V sensors).

Base-map logic to implement (from your notes)

Idle-up via closed-loop idle (absorbs AC load, no AC input); inverse-VSS idle-up for EPS current draw; AC-kill by load/throttle/boost; heat-soak comp from dual IAT (manifold Temp 2 + charge-pipe Temp 4); flat-shift; traction from wheel speeds (AWD — front-axle vs rear-axle slip); reverse switch → PCLink Trigger sets Gear = 7 (R) on 0x3EB so the cluster shows "R" (and RealDash's reverse-camera view). (Tune functions, not pins.)

Harness-build tasks

  1. Loom B +5V jumper. Official B-loom drawing has no +5V pin. Coolant pressure (B23), fuel level (B24), oil/fuel pressure (B15/B16) need 5 V jumpered from A32. Turbo speed is on DI 1 (A loom) — confirm its 5 V is local before running a jumper.
  2. Cam Hall pull-up. Fit 1.8 kΩ from signal to +8 V Out (A6). Do not fit the 2.4 kΩ in the RacerX kit.
  3. B14. Leave empty — no Superseal terminal.
  4. Shields. Terminate at the ECU end only (A7 / B17). Shields carry no current.
  5. Sensor grounds. Gnd Out only (A24 / B22), never chassis. BRZ APS GND1 and GND2 both return to Gnd Out; TPS may share that rail.
  6. ECU Hold Power. Aux 6 (A28) holds the EFI main relay. Ignition Switch sense is DI 9 (B28) from a dummy OEM ignition-switch block (key-on 12 V). OEM switch internals are not modelled.
  7. OEM blocks. Relays, bulkheads, and body-side switches/triggers (clutch, cruise stalk, brake, reverse, start, ignition switch) are generic OEM blocks on every drawing — do not invent OEM pinouts. The BRZ e-throttle pedal is the exception: it is a known 6-pin Sumitomo TS 025.
  8. Aux 7 MRS. Scope the speed-out high level before adding a 5 V pull-up.
